        Floor plan calibration
        1. Pick or add a floor, then load its floor plan image (any community map works).
        2. Tap a door marker, then tap the same door on the image.
        3. Repeat with a second door far from the first. The markers and room outlines snap onto the image.
        4. Press Save map. Add a 3rd door if things look mirrored or stretched.

        No door near the spot you want? Stand there in game and tap it on the image instead.

          If the plan is the wrong size, tilted or squashed, fit it to the door markers here. The markers stay put; the image moves. Lower "See-through" to see both.

          Rooms
          1. Tap the corners of a room on the map, in order.
          2. Press Finish room and name it. Doors on its walls are linked automatically.
          3. Press Save map when done.
